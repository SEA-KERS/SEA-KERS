# Surface brief: home (single-page showcase)

Mode: Persuade — the visitor must finish the page trusting SEA-KERS'
credibility and act (open details, reach out). Audience: recruiters,
sponsors, hackathon partners, prospective builders. Job: verify the team is
world-class; understand what it builds and why.

Content/proof: aspirational preview wins, projects, and roster (per
PRODUCT.md) — present with conviction, never as verified claims. The
particle caret mark is the brand centerpiece.

Direction: Magazine cover split. White editorial ground, hairline rules,
oversized Space Grotesk headlines, crimson as the only saturated accent;
cinematic navy #030624 reserved for the particle tile and the closing dark
chapters. Sections alternate composition: hero cover → dark record band →
alternating win spreads → featured spreads + index → staggered portraits +
masthead → dark story close → colophon.

Memorable moment: the particle caret cloud on its navy tile, floating over
an offset crimson block in the first viewport.

Unresolved: real win/roster data and future intake channel when the owner
supplies them.
